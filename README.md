# 🤠 Simple Steeds
**(Mod ID: `simplesteeds`)**

![Icon](src/main/resources/assets/simplesteeds/icon.png)

**Simple Steeds** revamps exploration with your trusty steed by introducing custom enchantments like **Momentum** and **Leaping** to saddles and armor while also allowing protection on them.

*Based on the mod [simpleriding](https://github.com/Ouzzi/simpleriding)*

---

## ✨ Core Feautures

* **Speed Boost:** Equip saddles with **Momentum** to amplify movement speeds across diverse mounts including horses, pigs, striders, camels, and happy ghasts.
* **Verticality:** Empower horse armor with **Leaping** to vault over barriers and scale high terrain with ease.
* **Defend Your Steed:** Horse Armor is no longer just cosmetic. It now accepts utility enchantments like **Feather Falling** and **Protection**.
* **Exploration Loot:** Find these rare enchantments in Bastions, Nether Fortresses, and Trial Chambers (Vaults).
* **Villager Trading:** Acquire the books directly through Master Librarians.

---

## 🔮 Enchantment Mechanics
Upgrading your mount requires rare books and an Anvil. Transform your ride into an all-terrain vehicle.

### Momentum (I - III)
* **Slot:** Saddles & Harness'
* **Function:** Increases movement speed by **30% per level** on Horses, Pigs, Striders and Camels and **50% per level** on Happy Ghasts. (Configurable).

### Leaping (I - III)
* **Slot:** Saddles
* **Function:** Increases jump strength by **10% per level** and **2.5% per level** for Camel leaps! (Configurable).

### Protection
* **Slot:** Horse Armor & Saddles
* **Function:** Integrates protective traits like Feather Falling, Frost Walker, Soul Speed and general protections directly onto saddles and horse armor.
* **Supports:** Feather Falling, Protection, Blast Protection, Fire Protection, Projectile Protection, Frost Walker and Soul Speed.

## 🛠️ In-Game Settings
* Access the built-in configuration UI straight through Mod Menu.
* Fine-tune toggle options for world generation and loot tables.
* Customize numerical balances for speeds, jumps, and damage reduction values.
